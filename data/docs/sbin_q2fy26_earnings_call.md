# State Bank of India - Q2 FY26 Earnings Call Transcript (Excerpt)
Ticker: SBIN.NS | Date: 2026-08-03 | Type: Earnings Call

Chairman remarks: Net interest income grew 9.6% year on year. Domestic net
interest margin held at 3.28%, broadly flat sequentially, despite continued
repricing of term deposits at higher rates.

Credit growth: Advances grew 14.2%, ahead of the banking system average.
Growth was led by SME and agriculture, with the retail unsecured book
deliberately held flat in response to regulatory guidance on risk weights.

Asset quality: Gross NPA fell to 2.05%, the lowest level in more than a decade,
with net NPA at 0.52%. The provision coverage ratio stood at 75.4%. Slippages
were Rs 4,900 crore, and management stated that the corporate book is showing
no signs of stress at present.

Costs: Operating profit was held back by provisions relating to the wage
revision settlement and pension liability. Management quantified the additional
provision at Rs 7,200 crore for the quarter and stated that the bulk of the
requirement has now been recognised.

Analyst Q&A on capital: Capital adequacy stood at 14.1%. Management stated that
internal accruals are sufficient to support the current growth trajectory and
that no equity raise is planned in the current financial year.

Analyst Q&A on valuation: Management declined to comment on the public-sector
valuation discount, noting only that return on assets has now exceeded 1% for
four consecutive quarters.
