# ITC Limited - Q2 FY26 Earnings Call Transcript (Excerpt)
Ticker: ITC.NS | Date: 2026-07-22 | Type: Earnings Call

CFO remarks: Gross revenue for the quarter grew 7.1% year on year. Segment
performance diverged sharply, with FMCG and Cigarettes offsetting weakness in
Agri Business.

FMCG (non-cigarette): Revenue grew 9.4% with segment EBITDA margin expanding
90 basis points to 11.2%. Management attributed the expansion to premiumisation
across the foods portfolio and moderating palm oil input costs. Eleven brands
now exceed Rs 1,000 crore in annual consumer spend.

Cigarettes: Net segment revenue grew 5.8% on volume growth of 2.4%. Management
noted that a stable taxation regime for a fourth consecutive year has allowed
volumes to recover from illicit trade, and that this stability is the single
largest determinant of segment performance.

Agri Business: Revenue declined 12% year on year, driven by export restrictions
on wheat and rice. Management described the impact as regulatory rather than
demand-driven and did not guide to a recovery timeline.

Hotels: Following the completion of the demerger, hotels no longer form part of
consolidated results from this quarter onward.

Analyst Q&A on capital allocation: Management reiterated a dividend payout
policy in the 80 to 85 percent range and confirmed no large inorganic
acquisitions are currently under evaluation.
