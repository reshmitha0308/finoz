# SEBI Regulation 30 Disclosure - Infosys Ltd
Ticker: INFY.NS | Date: 2026-07-16 | Type: Regulatory Filing

The Company informs the Exchanges that a large client in the Banking, Financial
Services and Insurance vertical has communicated a reduction in its committed annual
technology spend with the Company.

The estimated impact is a reduction of approximately USD 180 million in annualised
revenue, representing approximately 0.9% of consolidated FY25 revenue. The reduction
takes effect from the current quarter.

The Company has revised its FY26 revenue growth guidance to 0.5% to 2.0% in constant
currency to reflect this development and broader demand conditions.

The Company clarifies that the client relationship continues and that the reduction
reflects the internal cost programme of the client rather than any service quality
matter. No penalty or claim has been raised against the Company.
