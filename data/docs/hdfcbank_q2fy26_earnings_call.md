# HDFC Bank Ltd - Q2 FY26 Earnings Call Transcript (Excerpt)
Ticker: HDFCBANK.NS | Date: 2026-07-19 | Type: Earnings Call

CFO remarks: Net interest income for the quarter grew 11.4% year on year to
Rs 31,200 crore. Net interest margin expanded to 3.6% from 3.44%, reflecting
continued repricing of the merged mortgage book at higher yields.

Deposits: Total deposits grew 16.2% YoY, ahead of advances growth of 13.1%.
This is the first quarter in six where deposit growth has outpaced credit
growth, and management described it as a deliberate rebalancing to bring the
credit-deposit ratio down toward 96%.

Asset quality: Gross NPA ratio was steady at 1.24%, with net NPA at 0.33%.
Slippages were concentrated in the unsecured retail book but remain within
guided ranges.

Analyst Q&A on regulation: Management acknowledged the Reserve Bank of India
has signalled higher risk weights on unsecured retail lending. The Bank
estimates a capital impact of roughly 40 basis points on CAR, which it
considers absorbable without any capital raise.

Costs: The cost-to-income ratio improved to 39.8% from 41.2% as merger
integration expenses tapered.
