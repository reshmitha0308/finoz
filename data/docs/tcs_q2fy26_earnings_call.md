# Tata Consultancy Services - Q2 FY26 Earnings Call Transcript (Excerpt)
Ticker: TCS.NS | Date: 2026-07-11 | Type: Earnings Call

CEO remarks: Revenue for the quarter was USD 7.92 billion, growth of 4.1% YoY in
constant currency. Total contract value of deals signed stood at USD 11.2 billion,
the second highest in the history of the Company, including one deal above USD 1 billion
with a European banking client.

Operating margin came in at 25.4%, up 90 basis points sequentially. The improvement
was driven by lower subcontractor costs and improved utilisation at 87.2%.

People: Attrition on a last-twelve-months basis fell to 12.1%, the lowest in eight
quarters. Headcount was broadly flat at 6.09 lakh employees; management indicated
hiring will remain calibrated to demand rather than anticipatory.

Outlook: Management declined to give formal FY26 guidance but stated that the deal
pipeline supports continued mid-single-digit constant currency growth. Discretionary
spending in North America remains soft; BFSI in Europe is showing early recovery.
