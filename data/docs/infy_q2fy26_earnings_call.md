# Infosys Ltd - Q2 FY26 Earnings Call Transcript (Excerpt)
Ticker: INFY.NS | Date: 2026-07-16 | Type: Earnings Call

CEO remarks: Revenue for the quarter was USD 4.81 billion, a decline of 0.8% YoY in
constant currency. We are revising our FY26 revenue growth guidance downward to a
range of 0.5% to 2.0%, from the earlier 2.5% to 4.5%.

The revision reflects three factors: continued weakness in discretionary technology
spending among North American clients, the scale-down of one large BFSI engagement,
and pricing pressure on renewals in the manufacturing vertical.

Operating margin was 20.1%, down 110 basis points YoY. Management stated that margin
defence measures including pyramid optimisation are underway but will take two to
three quarters to show effect.

Analyst Q&A on the BFSI client: Management confirmed the client has reduced its
committed spend by approximately USD 180 million on an annualised basis, effective
from the current quarter. This is reflected in the revised guidance.

Positive note: AI and data services bookings grew 34% YoY, though from a small base,
and now represent 9% of total bookings.
