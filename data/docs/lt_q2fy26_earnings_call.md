# Larsen & Toubro Limited - Q2 FY26 Earnings Call Transcript (Excerpt)
Ticker: LT.NS | Date: 2026-07-25 | Type: Earnings Call

CFO remarks: Consolidated revenue grew 16.4% year on year. Order inflow for the
quarter was Rs 89,400 crore, taking the consolidated order book to a record
Rs 5.1 lakh crore, which represents approximately 2.4 years of trailing revenue.

Order composition: Infrastructure accounted for 61% of inflows, with the balance
from Energy and Hi-Tech Manufacturing. International orders were 48% of the
consolidated order book, the majority originating from the Middle East.

Margins: Core EBITDA margin was 9.1%, and management trimmed full-year guidance
by 20 basis points. The revision reflects cost pressure on a small number of
large international projects where fixed-price contracts were signed before
recent input cost movements. Management stated that the affected projects
represent under 8% of the order book and that domestic project margins are
holding.

Working capital: Net working capital to sales improved to 12.8% from 14.9%,
which management described as the principal driver of improved return on equity.

Analyst Q&A on strategy: Management reiterated the asset-light strategy, stating
that no new concession or build-own-operate assets are being pursued, and
confirmed the intention to maintain a higher dividend payout ratio alongside
buybacks where valuations permit.
