# SEBI Regulation 30 Disclosure - Tata Consultancy Services Ltd
Ticker: TCS.NS | Date: 2026-07-12 | Type: Regulatory Filing

The Board of Directors at its meeting held today approved a proposal to buy back
up to 4,09,63,855 equity shares of the Company at a price of Rs 4,150 per share,
aggregating to Rs 17,000 crore, through the tender offer route.

The buyback price represents a premium of approximately 14.6% over the closing price
on the day preceding the Board meeting. The buyback comprises approximately 1.12% of
the total paid-up equity capital.

The promoter group has indicated its intention to participate in the buyback.

The Company confirms it has sufficient distributable reserves and that the buyback
will be funded entirely from internal accruals without recourse to borrowings.
Post-buyback, the Company expects to retain a net cash position in excess of
Rs 55,000 crore.
