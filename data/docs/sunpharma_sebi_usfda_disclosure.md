# SEBI Regulation 30 Disclosure - Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd
Ticker: SUNPHARMA.NS | Date: 2026-08-05 | Type: Regulatory Filing

The Company informs the Exchanges that the United States Food and Drug
Administration concluded an inspection of the Company's manufacturing facility
at Halol, Gujarat, and issued a Form 483 containing observations.

The observations relate primarily to equipment cleaning validation and
investigation of out-of-specification results. The Company states that none of
the observations relate to data integrity.

The Company will respond to the observations within the stipulated period and
is committed to resolving them at the earliest. Pending resolution, approvals
for new products filed from this facility may be delayed. The facility
contributed approximately 6% of consolidated revenue in FY25.

Accompanying operational disclosure: the global specialty portfolio crossed
USD 1 billion in annual revenue for the first time, led by Ilumya and Winlevi.
Specialty now represents approximately 19% of consolidated revenue and carries
materially higher gross margins than the generics portfolio.

The Company notes that price erosion in the US generics market continued in
the mid single digits during the period, consistent with prior quarters, and
that the India formulations business grew 11%, ahead of the representative
market growth rate.
