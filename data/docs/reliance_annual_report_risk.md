# Reliance Industries Ltd - Annual Report FY25, Risk Factors Section (Excerpt)
Ticker: RELIANCE.NS | Date: 2026-05-30 | Type: Annual Report

Commodity price risk: The O2C business remains exposed to Singapore gross refining
margin benchmarks. A USD 1 per barrel movement in GRM affects segment EBITDA by
approximately Rs 3,100 crore annually.

Regulatory risk: Telecom tariff regulation and spectrum pricing decisions by TRAI
directly affect Jio profitability. The Company has no ability to forecast such changes.

Concentration risk: The top five customers of the O2C segment account for 22% of
segment revenue. Loss of any single relationship would not be material individually.

Currency risk: Approximately 58% of consolidated revenue is denominated in or linked
to USD, providing a partial natural hedge against import costs.
