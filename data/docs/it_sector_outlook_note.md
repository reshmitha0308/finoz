# Indian IT Services - Sector Outlook Note
Ticker: TCS.NS, INFY.NS | Date: 2026-08-10 | Type: Sector Research Note

Demand environment: Discretionary technology spending in North America remains
subdued for a fifth consecutive quarter. Deal activity has shifted decisively toward
cost-takeout and vendor-consolidation mandates, which carry longer conversion cycles
and lower initial pricing than transformation deals.

Divergence within the sector is widening. Vendors with strong large-deal execution
and cost discipline are protecting margins; vendors dependent on discretionary
project revenue are seeing guidance cuts.

Currency: A weaker rupee against the dollar provides roughly 30-40 basis points of
margin tailwind per 1% depreciation, partially offsetting pricing pressure.

Valuation: The sector trades at a modest discount to its five-year average forward
earnings multiple. We note that this discount is not uniform and reflects genuine
divergence in earnings visibility rather than a broad sector de-rating.

AI services: Bookings are growing rapidly across all major vendors but remain under
10% of total bookings. Revenue contribution is not yet material enough to offset
weakness in traditional service lines.
