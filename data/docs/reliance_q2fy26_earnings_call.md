# Reliance Industries Ltd - Q2 FY26 Earnings Call Transcript (Excerpt)
Ticker: RELIANCE.NS | Date: 2026-07-18 | Type: Earnings Call

CFO opening remarks: Consolidated revenue for the quarter stood at Rs 2.41 lakh crore,
up 9.2% year on year. EBITDA margin expanded 60 basis points to 17.8%, driven primarily
by the Retail segment.

Retail: Revenue grew 18% YoY. We added 340 stores this quarter, taking the total to
19,180. Digital commerce contributed 21% of segment revenue, up from 17%.

Jio: ARPU improved to Rs 208 from Rs 195. However, net subscriber additions were
6.1 million against 8.4 million in the comparable quarter, reflecting a maturing base.

O2C: Segment EBITDA declined 4% YoY on weaker refining margins. Management flagged that
crude price volatility remains the single largest swing factor for the coming two quarters.

Analyst Q&A on leverage: Net debt stands at Rs 1.18 lakh crore. Management reiterated
that new energy capex will be funded largely through internal accruals and does not
require incremental external borrowing at current levels.
