# Bharti Airtel Limited - Q2 FY26 Earnings Call Transcript (Excerpt)
Ticker: BHARTIARTL.NS | Date: 2026-07-29 | Type: Earnings Call

Managing Director remarks: Consolidated revenue grew 13.2% year on year.
India mobile average revenue per user rose to Rs 254 from Rs 233, the highest
among Indian operators, driven by the tariff revision taken last year and by
continued upgrading of subscribers to higher-value plans.

5G: The subscriber base on 5G devices crossed 120 million. Management stated
that 5G is now being monetised through data-heavy plan upgrades rather than a
separate premium, and that capital expenditure on 5G rollout has passed its
peak.

Africa: Revenue grew 14.1% in constant currency terms, with mobile money
revenue growing 26%. Management flagged that reported growth remains sensitive
to Nigerian naira movements and that hedging is only partial.

Balance sheet: Net debt to EBITDA stood at 2.4 times. Management confirmed that
deferred spectrum payment obligations remain the largest single component of
gross debt, and that prepayment of high-cost tranches continues where cash
flows permit.

Analyst Q&A on tariffs: Management declined to comment on the timing of any
further tariff revision, stating only that current returns on capital employed
in the India business remain below the level required to justify the invested
capital base.
