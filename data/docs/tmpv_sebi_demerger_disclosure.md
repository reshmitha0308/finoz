# SEBI Regulation 30 Disclosure - Tata Motors Ltd
Ticker: TMPV.NS (formerly TATAMOTORS.NS) | Date: 2026-06-28 | Type: Regulatory Filing

The Company informs the Exchanges that the scheme of arrangement for the
demerger of the Commercial Vehicles business into a separate listed entity
has received all required approvals and became effective from the record
date stated herein.

Shareholders received one share of the demerged commercial vehicle entity for
every one share held in the Company. The passenger vehicle, electric vehicle
and Jaguar Land Rover businesses remain within the existing listed entity.

Operational disclosure accompanying this filing: Jaguar Land Rover wholesale
volumes for the quarter declined 9.1% year on year, driven primarily by
softer demand in the China market and a planned model changeover. Management
notes that JLR contributes approximately 68% of consolidated revenue, and
that a sustained volume decline in that segment would be material to
consolidated earnings.

Domestic electric vehicle volumes crossed 25,000 units in the quarter, a
record for the Company, though EV contribution to consolidated revenue
remains under 6%.

Risk factors: input cost inflation in steel and aluminium continued to
compress commercial vehicle margins during the period prior to the demerger
becoming effective.
