# SEBI Regulation 30 Disclosure - Reliance Industries Ltd
Ticker: RELIANCE.NS | Date: 2026-08-02 | Type: Regulatory Filing

Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)
Regulations, 2015, the Company informs the Exchanges that the Board of Directors has
approved a capital expenditure programme of Rs 75,000 crore over 36 months for the
New Energy business, covering solar giga-factory expansion and battery storage capacity.

Funding structure: approximately 70% from internal accruals, 30% from already-sanctioned
credit facilities. No fresh equity dilution is contemplated.

Risk factors disclosed: execution timelines are dependent on regulatory clearances in
Gujarat and Andhra Pradesh. The Company notes that a delay beyond twelve months in
either clearance could defer revenue recognition from the segment into FY28.

The Company confirms that this disclosure contains no unpublished price-sensitive
information beyond what is stated herein.
